Account Deletion Request

You can request permanent deletion of your TDFGIP App account and associated data. All deletion requests are reviewed by our team before processing.

🕑

Review Period: 7-8 Business Days

All account deletion requests are stored and reviewed within 7-8 business days. During this period your account will be marked as "pending deletion." You may contact us to cancel the request before it is processed.

How to Request Deletion

  1. 1 Open the TDFGIP App on your device and log in to your account.
  2. 2 Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
  3. 3 Verify your identity using your registered mobile number via OTP.
  4. 4 Confirm and submit your account deletion request.

Alternatively, you can email us at admindesk@tdfjewellery.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" from the email or phone number linked to your account.

Data That Will Be Deleted

Permanently Deleted
  • Personal profile information (name, email, phone number)
  • Login credentials and authentication tokens
  • KYC documents and verification data
  • Push notification tokens and device information
  • App preferences and settings

Data That May Be Retained

Retained for Compliance
  • Transaction and payment records (up to 8 years)
  • Savings scheme records and instalment history (up to 8 years)
  • Invoice and billing records (as per tax regulations)

Retained data is required under the Income Tax Act and GST regulations of India. It will be anonymized where possible and used only for legal and compliance obligations. Once the retention period expires, this data will also be permanently deleted.
